How the number was reached

Nine managers played, so finishing first in a game is worth 9 points and finishing ninth is worth 1. In other words points = 10 − your place. Add your Kahoot points to your Quiplash points. Highest total picks earliest.

Raw scores are deliberately not added together. Kahoot pays around a thousand points per question and Quiplash pays a hundred per vote, so summing them would let the trivia decide the whole draft and make the second game decorative. Using places gives both games identical weight.
