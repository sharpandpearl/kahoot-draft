Part 1 · The Kahoot

9:30 – 9:45
Say — opening

Alright. Gentlemen, Megan. Welcome to the 2026 draft order challenge.

Under MAD DIC DOC, the reigning champion proposes the challenge. I am the reigning champion. So this is the challenge. You had forty-eight hours to overrule me and nobody did.

Two games tonight. First a trivia round. Then a second game where you write your own answers and we all vote on them. Where you finish in each one counts equally.

And what you are playing for is not your draft slot. It is the order in which you choose your draft slot. Which matters, because if you are sitting on two first-round keepers you do not want the 1.01, and you know who you are.

(Beat.) Steve Shuldman does not have to play. Steve went to a Waffle House for twenty-four hours. Steve picks first no matter what happens in the next half hour. That is the system working as designed.

Start the Kahoot

  1. On the Kahoot tab, click Start, then choose Classic mode — individual players, not teams.
  2. Open game options and set two things. Turn Answer streak bonus off. Turn Show questions and answers on players' devices on.
  3. The game PIN appears. Read it out and put it in the WhatsApp thread for the remote guys.
  4. Wait until all twelve names are in the lobby before you start.
The second setting is the important one. Without it, phones show only four coloured shapes and the remote players are reading questions off a laggy screenshare — while Kahoot breaks ties on answer speed. If someone says they cannot read the question, the gear icon at the bottom right of the host screen toggles it mid-game.
Say — before you hit start

Everyone go to kahoot dot it and put in the PIN. Use your real name. I have to type the finishing order into the second game afterwards, and I am not deciding which one of you is Dick Dock Frequent Flyer.

Twenty questions. You get points for right answers and for answering fast, so ties get broken by your thumbs. That is a feature. Take it up with the commissioner.

Some of this is football. Some of it is our own bylaws. If you have never read the bylaws, this is the part of the evening where that becomes your problem.

Part 2 · The app

9:45 – 9:57
  1. Switch the TV to the app tab. It shows the join address in large type.
  2. Send the plain URL to WhatsApp. Everyone opens it and taps their own name.
  3. Join on your own phone too — the laptop is the screen, not a player.
  4. When all twelve chips are showing, hit Start round 1 in the bar at the bottom.
Say — explaining the format

Second game. Open the link on your phone and tap your name.

A prompt comes up on the TV. You get thirty seconds to type an answer. Then everybody sees all twelve answers with no names on them, and you vote for your two favourites.

Every vote you get is a hundred points. You cannot vote for yourself — I had that specifically disabled, because I know this league.

Funniest wins. Not truest. Although historically those have overlapped.

Repeat for each round
  1. Prompt appears with a thirty-second clock. It turns red at ten seconds.
  2. Anything typed when it hits zero submits automatically — nobody loses an answer to the buzzer.
  3. Click Open voting. Answers appear anonymously; everyone picks two on their phone.
  4. When the counter says everyone has voted, click Show results — authors and vote counts revealed, standings updated.
  5. Read the winner out loud with the author's name. That is the whole point of the game.
  6. Click Next round.
Say — between rounds, as needed

Whoever wrote that, you are a sick individual and you are moving up the board.

Zero votes. Zero. That is going in the group chat.

(If a round dies flat.) We are never speaking of that round again. Next.

Four rounds is the plan; there are ten loaded. End & final order works after any round, so read the room. If it is flying, keep going. If people are drifting, end it and move to the reveal — a dying game is worse than a short one.

Part 3 · The reveal

9:57 – 10:00
  1. Click End & final order.
  2. Click Enter Kahoot results in the host bar.
  3. With your Kahoot screenshot open, tap the twelve names in the order they finished the Kahoot, winner first. Undo last fixes a misclick.
  4. Click Save order. The TV switches to the combined selection order.
Say — while you type the order in

Give me thirty seconds, I am entering the Kahoot results.

Here is the maths, so nobody accuses me of anything. Finishing first in a game is worth twelve points, second is eleven, all the way down. You add your two finishes together. Both games count exactly the same amount.

We are not adding the raw scores, because Kahoot hands out about a thousand points a question and the second game hands out a hundred a vote. Add those together and the trivia decides everything and the funny round is decoration. So it is placings.

Say — the reveal

On the screen. Each line shows your total, then your Kahoot placing and your placing in the second game, so you can check my working.

Steve Shuldman, selection number one. He ate his way out of a Waffle House and this is what it bought him. Respect.

(Read from twelve upwards. Make them wait.)

And that is binding. Screenshot it.

Part 4 · Choosing slots

10:00 onward
Say — setting up the selection

Now we go in that order and you each claim a draft slot. Not a pick — a slot. One through twelve, first come first served.

Think about your keepers before you open your mouth. If you are keeping two guys at first and second round cost, the 1.01 is worth almost nothing to you and a middle slot with better snake position is the actual prize.

Steve, you are up. Take your time. You have earned exactly this much.

Write each choice on the draft board as it is called. Once a slot is taken it is gone.

If something breaks

Phone shows only shapesKahoot gear icon, bottom right of the host screen → turn on show questions and answers on players' devices.
Someone drops out of the appThey reopen the link and tap their name again. The seat is reclaimed by name and their score is intact.
Someone joined as the wrong personHit Reset, everyone rejoins. Faster than arguing about it. Only do this before round one.
A phone is stuck on an old screenRefresh the page. It polls every second and a half and will catch up on its own.
Nothing is advancingNothing advances by itself except the thirty-second answer clock. You drive every other step from the host bar.
You lost the Kahoot leaderboardSkip Part 3 step 2. The app falls back to its own scores alone. Say so out loud rather than quietly.
